---
title: Deliver multiple cards automatically
description: Run a delivery workflow over an epic.
---

## Steps

Run `pair-cli run delivery --root <epic> --parallel 3` from the project checkout, or ask your assistant to run `/pair-workflow-run delivery --root <epic>`.

## Verify

The workflow selects eligible cards and runs independent cards in parallel. A dependent card waits until its prerequisites merge. If a prerequisite needs approval, the summary names it and explains how to resume.

Without `--card`, `--root`, or `--filter`, the CLI previews the selection and asks for confirmation. Without a terminal, it prints the selection and launches nothing.
