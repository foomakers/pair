---
title: Measure delivery
description: Generate the delivery and AI metrics retro panel for a period.
---

**Goal**: see bug resolution time, PR lead time and process adoption.

## Prerequisites

- A PM tool and code host configured

## Steps

1. **Run the skill**
   - In your assistant: `/pair-capability-analyze-delivery-metrics` for the period you want.
2. **Read the panel**
   - It is written under `.pair/working/reports/`.
3. **Use it in the retro**
   - Pick one metric to improve; re-run next period and compare.

## Verify

- A dated panel exists under `reports/`.
