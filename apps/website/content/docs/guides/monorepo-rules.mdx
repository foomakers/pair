---
title: Package-local rules in a monorepo
description: Keep per-package adoption rules next to the package while the central files stay the router.
---

**Goal**: let each package carry its own architecture, testing or code-design rules, and let skills load only the packages a diff or story touches.

## Prerequisites

- A monorepo with pair installed
- The central adoption files in `.pair/adoption/tech/`

## Steps

1. **Wait for, or request, the proposal**
   - In your assistant: skills never activate the override on their own. When `review`, `assess-*` or `refine-story` see three or more packages with recurring package-specific rules, or a central file past 400 lines, they propose a scaffold plan (which packages, which rules move, the router to add) and wait for your approval. You can also ask for the plan directly.
   - With `/pair-assistant` (optional, Claude Code): "add package-local rules for apps/web".
2. **Declare the router**
   - In your assistant: add a `## Package-Local Adoption` table to the central `tech/<area>.md` with one row per package: `Package | Local context | Areas`, for example `apps/web | apps/web/.pair/ | architecture, testing`.
3. **Create the package files**
   - In your assistant: write `<pkg>/.pair/<area>.md` for each listed package and area, holding only the package-specific rules. Global rules stay central.
   - With `/pair-assistant` (optional, Claude Code): shows each file before writing.
4. **Move, do not duplicate**
   - A rule present both centrally and locally is reported as a conflict by `review` and `assess-*`, never resolved silently. Delete the central copy when a rule becomes local.

## Verify

- A review of a diff in `apps/web` loads the global rules plus `apps/web/.pair/`, and nothing from other packages.
- `pair-cli validate-config` still passes: local `.pair/` directories are project content, never a registry target.
