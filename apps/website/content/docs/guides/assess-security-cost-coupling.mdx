---
title: Assess security, cost and coupling
description: Run the output-only assessment skills on a diff or the whole codebase.
---

**Goal**: get a security, cost or coupling verdict without changing any file.

## Prerequisites

- pair skills installed

## Steps

1. **Security**
   - In your assistant: `/pair-capability-assess-security $mode: review` on a PR, or `$mode: audit` for a one-shot OWASP scan.
2. **Cost**
   - In your assistant: `/pair-capability-assess-cost` gives a chromatic cost class for a change.
3. **Coupling**
   - In your assistant: `/pair-capability-assess-coupling $scope: diff` for a PR, or `$scope: full` for an audit.
4. **Act on the findings**
   - Promote tolerable ones to tech debt; fix critical ones before merge. The review runs the same three assessments and shows each as a one-line verdict.

## Verify

- Reports for audits land in `.pair/working/reports/`.
