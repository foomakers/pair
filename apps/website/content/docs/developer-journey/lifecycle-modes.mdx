title: Lifecycle Modes
description: Three ways to run the pair lifecycle, Assisted, Semi-automatic and Automatic, across the refinement and delivery workflows, and when to use which.
---

The nine-step lifecycle and its two workflows ([analysis and refinement](/docs/developer-journey/refinement-workflow), [delivery](/docs/developer-journey/delivery-workflow)) are the same in every mode. What changes is **who drives it**: you, step by step; a delivery workflow you trigger per card; or a loop that runs unattended. Pick per project, or per card.

![Three lifecycle modes compared across the refinement and delivery workflows](/diagrams/lifecycle-modes.svg)

*Figure: rows are the two workflows, columns the three modes.*

## Assisted

The classic mode: **you run every phase explicitly and the AI assists at each step.**

1. [Create the PRD](/docs/developer-journey/induction) (`/pair-process-specify-prd`)
2. [Bootstrap](/docs/developer-journey/induction#step-2-bootstrap-the-project) (`/pair-process-bootstrap`)
3. [Plan initiatives, epics and stories](/docs/developer-journey/strategic-planning) (`/pair-process-plan-initiatives`, `-plan-epics`, `-plan-stories`)
4. [Refine and plan tasks](/docs/developer-journey/iteration) (`/pair-process-refine-story`, `/pair-process-plan-tasks`)
5. [Implement and review](/docs/developer-journey/execution) (`/pair-process-implement`, `/pair-process-review`)

**Use it when** you are learning pair, the project is new or high-risk, or you want to read and approve every decision.

## Semi-automatic

**You steer the planning, a delivery workflow runs the stages and stops at checkpoints for you.**

- **Bootstrap**: `/pair-process-bootstrap`, optionally in [quick mode](/docs/getting-started/bootstrap-quick-mode), which reads your project instead of asking.
- **Refinement**: by grilling and triage: `/pair-capability-grill` interviews you one question at a time, `/pair-process-brainstorm` turns a theme into a Draft backlog tree.
- **Implementation**: through the [delivery workflow](/docs/developer-journey/delivery-workflow): you trigger `/pair-workflow-cycle` per card, or `/pair-implement-batch` for a batch. The workflow orchestrates implement, independent verify and remediation, and stops at checkpoints (the review-approved PR, any escalation) for you.

**Use it when** the project is set up, the backlog is known, and you want independent verification without running each stage by hand. Details: [Delivery cycle](/docs/reference/delivery-cycle#3-three-realizations-and-which-to-use).

## Automatic

**Pair selects cards and delivers them without you, within the gates you set.**

- **Drivers**: `/pair-loop` in a session, or `pair-cli run --root <id> --parallel <n> --watch` as a daemon.
- **Gates**: `until` (`ready`, `pr`, `merged`), `prepare` and `merge` decide how far a card goes; an `escalation` hands a card back to a human.
- **Safe defaults**: nothing merges and no Draft card is prepared unattended until you declare it.

**Use it when** classification and gates are trusted and the backlog is mostly low-risk. Walkthrough: [Automated development](/docs/tutorials/automated-development#autonomy-until-gates-escalation); every key: [Adoption settings](/docs/reference/adoption-settings#techautomationmd).

## Modes by workflow

| Mode | Workflow | Who triggers | What runs | Where it stops | Config |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Assisted | Refinement | you, per skill | `/pair-process-brainstorm`, `-plan-stories`, `-refine-story`, `-plan-tasks` | after each skill | none |
| Assisted | Delivery | you, per skill | `/pair-process-implement`, `/pair-process-review` | after each skill | none |
| Semi-automatic | Refinement | you, per batch | `/pair-refine-batch`, `pair-cli run --until ready` | Ready, or a grill question | `prepare` |
| Semi-automatic | Delivery | you, per card or batch | `/pair-workflow-cycle`, `/pair-implement-batch` | review-approved PR, escalation | `until`, Blocking Severities |
| Automatic | Refinement | the loop | `prepare` gate `never` or `when` | escalation (`needs-review`) | `prepare` |
| Automatic | Delivery | the loop or daemon | `/pair-loop`, `pair-cli run --root --parallel --watch` | gates `until` and `merge`, escalation | `## Autonomy` |

Every key: [Adoption settings](/docs/reference/adoption-settings#techautomationmd).

## Which to use

| Situation | Mode |
| --- | --- |
| New to pair, new project, high-risk work | Assisted |
| Known backlog, you want to trigger and review per card | Semi-automatic |
| Mature gates, trusted low-risk backlog, no one watching | Automatic |

Modes mix: plan Assisted, deliver Semi-automatic, and hand the green cards to Automatic.

**With the CLI:** Assisted and Semi-automatic use skills in your assistant; Automatic is `pair-cli run`. **With `/pair-assistant` (optional, Claude Code):** ask it to configure the gates ([how to ask](/docs/reference/pair-assistant#how-to-ask)).
