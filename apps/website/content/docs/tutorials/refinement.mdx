title: Refinement
description: Take one user story from Draft to Ready, grill sync, refine, plan tasks, and read the Assumptions and Open Questions it leaves on the card.
---

**Goal:** one Draft story becomes Ready: acceptance criteria, analysis, classification, sizing and tasks. About 30 minutes. Second step of the [analysis and refinement workflow](/docs/developer-journey/refinement-workflow); the story can come from [Brainstorming](/docs/tutorials/brainstorming).

## 1. Refine

```text
/pair-process-refine-story #88
```

**Phase 0, sync.** `/pair-capability-grill` interviews you one question at a time (goal, edge cases, dependencies, risks). No section is written until you and the AI agree. This is a blocking gate.

Then the skill writes Given-When-Then acceptance criteria, the technical analysis, a [classification](/docs/reference/quality-model) and a size, and moves the card toward Ready. Detail: [Iteration](/docs/developer-journey/iteration#step-6-refine-a-user-story).

**With the CLI:** `pair-cli run --card 88 --prepare never --until ready` refines alone and stops before implementation. **With `/pair-assistant` (optional, Claude Code):** CLI only for the run; ask [`/pair-assistant`](/docs/reference/pair-assistant#how-to-ask) to set the `prepare` gate.

## 2. Plan tasks

```text
/pair-process-plan-tasks #88
```

Adds a task checklist, a dependency graph and an AC coverage table to the card. The card is now `Ready` ([canonical states](/docs/concepts/canonical-states)).

## 3. Read Assumptions and Open Questions

Two sections on the card record what the interview could not settle:

- **Assumptions**: a question, the answer the AI chose, and what would overturn it. Skim them; reject any you disagree with.
- **Open Questions**: unresolved items. Resolve or accept each before delivery.

When the `prepare` gate lets pair refine alone ([autonomy](/docs/tutorials/automated-development#autonomy-until-gates-escalation)), these sections are your review surface.

## What you have

A Ready story. Next: [Automated development](/docs/tutorials/automated-development), or `/pair-process-implement` by hand ([Execution](/docs/developer-journey/execution)).
