These pages explain **how pair works**. Read them when you want understanding, not steps; the steps are in [Guides](/docs/guides) and the flow in the [Process Lifecycle](/docs/process-lifecycle). Every page ends with a Next link, so the whole section reads top to bottom.

## Core

Start here if you are new to pair.

1. **[AI-assisted SDLC](/docs/concepts/ai-assisted-sdlc)**: the problem pair solves and how structured context changes AI collaboration end to end.
2. **[Knowledge Base](/docs/concepts/knowledge-base)**: the documents installed into `.pair/` that give your assistant persistent context.
3. **[Skills](/docs/concepts/skills)**: the actions on top, structured instructions your assistant discovers and runs.
4. **[Adoption files](/docs/concepts/adoption-files)**: your project's decisions, recorded so they never drift.
