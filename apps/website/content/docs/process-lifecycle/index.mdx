The lifecycle is the spine of working with pair. Four [phases](/docs/process-lifecycle/phases) take a product from idea to merged code; two workflows run on top of them; three modes decide who drives; a stack of skills does the work. Every page in this section links out to the guide that configures a thing and the reference that documents its commands.

![The process lifecycle: four phases, two workflows, three modes](/diagrams/lifecycle-overview.svg)

*Figure: read it left to right. The phases are the ground; the two workflows sit on the last two phases; the three modes change who drives, never what runs.*

## The four parts

- **Phases**: [induction](/docs/process-lifecycle/phases/induction) (one-time setup), [strategic planning](/docs/process-lifecycle/phases/strategic-planning), [iteration](/docs/process-lifecycle/phases/iteration) and [execution](/docs/process-lifecycle/phases/execution). Nine numbered steps, each with a how-to guide and a skill. See [Phases](/docs/process-lifecycle/phases).
- **Two workflows**: the [analysis and refinement workflow](/docs/process-lifecycle/refinement-workflow) turns an idea into a Ready [card](/docs/concepts/glossary#card); the [delivery workflow](/docs/process-lifecycle/delivery-workflow) turns a Ready card into a review-approved pull request. [In depth](/docs/process-lifecycle/delivery-cycle).
- **Three modes**: Assisted (pair guides you through every phase), Supervised (pair automates one card end to end) and Autonomous (pair watches the backlog and processes every card matching your criterion), like the levels of driving automation in cars. Same phases, same workflows; what changes is who drives. See [Lifecycle modes](/docs/process-lifecycle/lifecycle-modes).
- **Skill layers**: capability skills at the bottom, process skills in the middle, workflow, loop and batch skills on top. See [Skill layers](/docs/process-lifecycle/skill-layers).

Ahead of the phases sits one optional entry point, before Induction: `/pair-process-brainstorm`, a structured discovery interview that turns a theme into a Draft epic and story tree. It never modifies the PRD.

## Let /pair-next guide you

You do not need to memorize the sequence. Run `/pair-next` at any point: pair reads your project state (open issues, story states, task progress) and suggests the next step. Use it at the start of every session, after finishing a step, or whenever you are unsure.

```text
/pair-next
```

## The risk-driven thread

Every story carries a **risk tier**, and the tier decides how much gate, review and merge ceremony its pull request gets. It enters at refinement (`/pair-capability-classify` writes the matrix and, when the project projects tags, the `risk:*` tag), is copied onto the PR by `publish-pr`, scales the pre-merge gate and the review, and filters what [tag-driven automation](/docs/process-lifecycle/tag-driven-automation) may pick up. Review may raise a tier, never lower it. The model lives in [Tag-driven quality gates](/docs/concepts/tag-driven-gates) and the [Quality model](/docs/reference/quality-model); switching it on is a guide: [Classify stories and use risk tags](/docs/guides/classify-risk).
