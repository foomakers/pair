Delivery cycle: a maintainer's verified decision comment resumes a parked run (#647). `cycle-state.mjs decisions` reads the card's and the PR's comments (one fenced `maintainer-decision` JSON block, parsed rigidly) and accepts only the adopted maintainer, authenticated by the host; `resolve` names every stop's `block` and consumes the newest decision for it (`decide`, `amend`, `override`, `reset`) — no `supersede` chain, no head move. Custody overrides are derived from verified override comments; a reset re-seals through the independent seal path. A finding overlapping a recorded decision parks `decision-conflict` before any preparation; a missing policy is read fail-closed, recorded as a contract assumption and an Assumption comment on the card. `pair run` picks decisions up before every resolve and prints the decision block. Breaking: `--maintainer` is no longer accepted by `apply-scope-decisions` and `approve-correction`.
