Implementation claims unassigned cards for the authenticated runner, including bots. Cards held by another owner are skipped unless explicitly overridden, and batches continue with other cards. Card creation and refinement no longer apply the default assignee. Pull requests and maintainer decisions still use their adopted settings.

Workflows can assign or clear ownership per step. Workflow configuration and CLI overrides take precedence, and `pair run` prints each effective value and its source.
